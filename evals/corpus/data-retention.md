# Data retention and deletion

## Retention periods

Invoices and payment records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and accounting rules. Application logs are kept for 30 days. Exported reports are deleted from our servers after 72 hours.

## Account deletion requests

A workspace owner can request deletion of the whole workspace from Settings > Workspace > Delete. Personal data is erased within 30 days of the request. Invoices are kept for the legal 7-year period even after deletion, with personal fields removed.

## Backups

Encrypted database backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 35 days. Deleted data can persist in backups until those backups expire.
