# Billing FAQ

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing period. Past invoices can be downloaded as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices.

## Payment methods

Ledgerly accepts Visa, Mastercard, and American Express cards, and SEPA direct debit for customers in the euro area. Enterprise customers can pay by bank transfer against an invoice with net 30 terms.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, we retry automatically after 3, 5, and 7 days and email the billing contact each time. After the final failed retry, the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid. Data is never deleted because of a failed payment.

## Taxes

Prices exclude VAT. EU business customers can add a valid VAT ID in Settings > Billing so that the reverse charge applies.
