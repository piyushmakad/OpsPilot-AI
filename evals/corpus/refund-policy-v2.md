# Refund policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original payment. Refunds always go back to the original payment method; we cannot refund to a different card or bank account. A payment that has already been refunded cannot be refunded again.

## Annual plans

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full during the first 30 days. After 30 days, an annual plan can be cancelled so it does not renew, but the remaining months are not refunded on a pro-rata basis.

## Enterprise contracts

Enterprise customers on a signed order form follow the refund terms in their contract. Support agents must not promise an enterprise refund; route the request to the account manager.

## Processing time

Approved refunds reach the customer within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank. Refunds above 100 USD require manager approval before they are issued.
